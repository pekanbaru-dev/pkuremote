# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People based in Pekanbaru who use the PKUBersua event site to find and join community events across professions. The audience is broad by design — designers, developers, writers, health workers, educators, makers — anyone in the city who wants to meet other locals at talks, workshops, and meetups. Their context: checking the site between meetings on a phone or laptop, looking for what's happening this week. The job to be done: quickly find the next event, see what's coming up, and book a seat.

## Product Purpose

PKUBersua is the public face of a Pekanbaru community-of-events. "Bersua" is Indonesian for "to meet" — the site exists so people in the city can find each other, show up to the same room, and learn something together. It surfaces upcoming and past events, gives each event its own page with a booking affordance, and signals to prospective attendees that the community is active and worth joining. Success looks like: a visitor lands, sees what's on this week, and books a seat — or sees the last event's photos and decides to come next time.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, focused. The site should feel like an inviting community notice board — not a loud event-aggregator or a busy social feed. Three-word personality: **warm, calm, focused**. The cream canvas and golden accent carry warmth; the editorial layout and restrained motion keep it calm; the content (real events, real dates, real names) keeps it focused. Voice is direct and low-key; copy is short and specific. Emotional goal: a visitor lands and feels "this is my kind of room."

Reference: a small community centre's notice board — generous whitespace, a single warm accent, real names, real dates, and one clear invitation. The feel to borrow: content is the hero, chrome stays out of the way, and a list of upcoming events reads as a quiet, trustworthy bulletin rather than a marketing grid.

## Anti-references

Not a generic website. Specifically avoid:

- Templated "community/club" WordPress layouts with crowded sidebars, event-countdown widgets, and stock illustrations.
- Loud SaaS-landing gradients, hero metric cards, and identical feature-card grids.
- Social-feed aesthetics (busy cards, reaction counts, infinite scroll).
- Anything that reads as a generic CMS theme rather than an edited publication.

## Design Principles

1. **Content is the hero.** Events lead; chrome recedes. Whitespace and type hierarchy carry the layout, not boxes and borders.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Real event names, real dates, real hosts. No "We are a vibrant community" filler.
3. **Calm by default.** One accent (golden), restrained motion, no decorative noise. Energy comes from the content's timeliness, not from the UI.
4. **Editorial discipline.** Each section earns its place; if a widget doesn't serve finding or booking, it's not on the page.
5. **Inclusive scope.** The community is lintas profesi by design — the site must read as welcoming to people across professions, not just one industry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: ≥4.5:1 body text contrast, semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable, visible focus.
- **Reduced motion**: every animation ships a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant).
- **Color-blind safe**: no color-only signals; pair color with text, icon, or pattern.
