# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Remote-working people based in Pekanbaru who use the PKU Remote club site to stay connected to their local professional community. Their context: working alone or from home, checking the site between tasks on a laptop or phone, looking for what's happening next. The job to be done: quickly find the next event, read the latest announcement, or catch up on club blog posts without wading through noise.

## Product Purpose

PKU Remote is the public face of a Pekanbaru remote-worker community. The site promotes club events, posts announcements, and publishes blog content so members can stay informed and prospective members can see the community is active and worth joining. Success looks like: a member opens the site, knows within seconds what's happening this week, and signs up for the next event or reads the latest post.

## Brand Personality

Calm, minimal, focused. The site should feel like a quiet, well-edited bulletin board — not a loud event-aggregator or a busy social feed. Three-word personality: **calm, minimal, focused**. Voice is direct and low-key; copy is short and specific (real dates, real names, real places). Emotional goal: a member lands and feels "this is my community, and it's in good hands."

Reference: **Substack** — its reading-first typography, generous whitespace, restrained color, and post-list rhythm. The feel to borrow: content is the hero, chrome stays out of the way, and a list of recent posts reads as a quiet, trustworthy feed rather than a marketing grid.

## Anti-references

Not a generic website. Specifically avoid:

- Templated "community/club" WordPress layouts with crowded sidebars, event-countdown widgets, and stock illustrations.
- Loud SaaS-landing gradients, hero metric cards, and identical feature-card grids.
- Social-feed aesthetics (busy cards, reaction counts, infinite scroll).
- Anything that reads as a generic CMS theme rather than an edited publication.

## Design Principles

1. **Content is the hero.** Events, announcements, and posts lead; chrome recedes. Whitespace and type hierarchy carry the layout, not boxes and borders.
2. **Show, don't tell.** Real event names, real dates, real authors. No "We are a vibrant community" filler.
3. **Calm by default.** One accent, restrained motion, no decorative noise. Energy comes from the content's timeliness, not from the UI.
4. **Editorial discipline.** Each section earns its place; if a widget doesn't serve finding or reading, it's not on the page.
5. **Expert confidence.** Quiet, well-typeset, and precise — the site itself is evidence the community is run by people who care about craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: ≥4.5:1 body text contrast, semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable, visible focus.
- **Reduced motion**: every animation ships a `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` alternative (crossfade or instant).
- **Color-blind safe**: no color-only signals; pair color with text, icon, or pattern.
